# Business Insights

## 1. Fraud Activity

Fraud represents a small proportion of total transaction activity, but fraudulent transactions can involve substantial transaction amounts.

## 2. Transaction Type Risk

CASH_OUT and TRANSFER transactions require closer monitoring because of their contribution to fraudulent activity.

## 3. High-Value Transactions

Transactions in the highest transaction-value percentile show elevated fraud risk compared with the overall transaction population.

## 4. Balance Anomalies

Balance inconsistencies provide an additional indicator that can be used to prioritize suspicious transactions.

## 5. Account Concentration

A relatively small number of origin and destination accounts contribute a significant portion of observed fraud amount.

## 6. Risk Categorization

The rule-based risk scoring framework can help prioritize transactions for investigation based on multiple risk indicators.
